A team of problem-solvers, innovators and partners

R3Hub was founded by experienced professionals who spent over a decade in IT infrastructure, engineering, system integration and software management systems. We shared a passion for making a difference in our field, and started our own company to do more of it. The name R3HUB reflects that commitment to high-quality services and solutions.

Our expertise covers data centers, power and cooling, network security, healthcare systems, ERP, auxiliary systems and other IT-related solutions.

From a single server room to a full command center, we handle the whole job: consultation, design, integration, installation, training and support.
